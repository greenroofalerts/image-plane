## Pass 1 — album joins (method `album_f2`)

Because keeps are exclusively iCloud paths, a no-coords keep can never literally BE a takeout
album row — the join has to be by content identity. `sha256` overlap between the two ledgers is
confirmed 0 (Takeout re-encodes on export, per the established finding), so the only surviving
join key is **basename** (the same weak signal already named in the skill notes, ~1.2k corpus-
wide overlap). Method: for each of the 809, look up its basename in
`takeout_ledger_merged.jsonl` rows carrying a non-null `job_ref` (the existing album->job_ref
parse — not re-derived).

- 803 of 809: no basename evidence at all (passed to Pass 2 untouched).
- 6 of 809: basename matched exactly one candidate `job_ref` (0 basename collisions across
  multiple distinct job_refs).
- Of those 6, one was **vetoed**: album `1790-25 Frimley Centre Install 3-26 TN37 6AS` implies a
  2025/26 job, but the candidate photo's own EXIF/path date is 2023-04-20 (2+ years off, no
  corroborating year token in the album name) — an IMG-counter-reuse false positive, not
  applied. The other 5 all had icloud-path dates falling inside the job's own install/diagnostic
  window (e.g. "Diagnostic 16-3" album vs 2026-03-17 photo date) — applied.

**Result: 5 new rows, method `album_f2`, confidence `strong`, date = icloud path date.** Row
shape matches the existing `album`/`album_label` precedent (`path`, `job_ref`, `method`,
`confidence`, `date`, `evidence`).

## Pass 2 — burst/filename adjacency (method `adjacency_f2`)

Remaining pool: 804 (809 minus the 5 Pass-1 matches; the 1 vetoed Pass-1 candidate re-enters
this pool since its album evidence was struck down, not "used up").

Method: got a precise timestamp per candidate (`mdls kMDItemContentCreationDate`, batched per
Mini A file, fallback to file mtime if EXIF absent, `missing_on_disk_no_exif` if the file isn't
present at all — 33 of 809 fall in `kept_missing_on_disk.json`, confirmed, flagged separately,
never guessed from path-day alone since day-granularity can't support a "within minutes" test).
Built a same-day pool of 1,907 already-allocated icloud photos (170 of the 390 distinct
candidate days had any allocated photo at all) and their own precise timestamps. Threshold: **15
minutes** (a judgement call, not in the spec verbatim — chosen as the outer edge of a plausible
same-visit burst window; matches actually found cluster far tighter: 8 at <=1 min, 50 at 1-5 min,
31 at 5-10 min, 17 at 10-15 min, none near a padded edge).

Decision rule per candidate: >=2 allocated photos of the SAME job within 15 min -> match
(`likely`). >=2 allocated photos each from >=2 DIFFERENT jobs within 15 min -> ambiguous,
residue (0 cases this run). Exactly 1 allocated photo of any job within 15 min -> single weak
signal, residue, not forced. 0 allocated photos of any job within 15 min on that day -> no
signal, residue.

**Result: 106 candidate matches, `adjacency_f2`, confidence `likely`.**

One of the 106 was the same photo Pass 1 vetoed (`.../2023/04/20/IMG_0253.HEIC`): its adjacency
signal independently pointed to `1592-23` (0.4/0.6 min from 2 allocated `1592-23` photos — date-
consistent with the photo's own 2023 EXIF, unlike the vetoed 1790-25 album guess). Two
disagreeing signals attached to one photo (one struck down, one live) is exactly the shape Lee
should see rather than have auto-resolved — **pulled out of auto-apply and put in the residue
file** with both pieces of evidence shown, instead of trusting the sequence. Net: **105 pass-2
rows actually applied.**

No `exact` band was ever used (hard rule: never exact without two independently-agreeing
signals; no case this run had two agreeing signals — the one case with two signals had them
disagree, so it went to residue instead of being upgraded).
